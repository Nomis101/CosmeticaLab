# CosmeticaLab

Professional cosmetic formulation, perfumery, and soap making — all in one native app. One-time purchase. No subscription. Your data stays on your device.


CosmeticaLab is the professional workstation for cosmetic formulators, perfumers, and soap makers. Built as a native macOS and iPadOS app, it delivers the performance and reliability you expect from a desktop-class tool — with seamless iCloud sync across all your devices.

**Cosmetic Formulation**
Create and manage cosmetic formulations with a comprehensive ingredient library backed by the EU CosIng database (34,500+ entries). Organize by phase, track percentages and costs, and generate EU-compliant INCI lists with nano-material labeling. Automated HLB calculation, comedogenicity scoring, and active substance monitoring keep your formulations on target. Export to PDF, CSV, XLSX, or BBCode.

**Perfumery**
Build fragrance formulations with a dedicated fragrance library, olfactory family classification, and IFRA compliance checking across three product categories. Explore ingredient pairings grounded in co-occurrence analysis and olfactory theory. Develop your personal Style DNA profile from your body of work. Browse reference data from The Good Scents Company database with 47,000+ substances and 18,000+ demo formulations.

**Soap Making**
Formulate cold-process and hot-process soaps with NaOH and KOH support. A database of 49 oils with full SAP values, 56 additives, and 213 colorants. The Soap Composer recommends oil blends matching your target properties — hardness, cleansing, conditioning, lather. Quality metrics, INCI generation, and cost calculation included.

**Formulation Tools**
Sixteen calculators and advisors: reference tables (ASM, comedogenicity, usage ranges); buffer pH (5 systems), HLB, foam, and solubilization calculators; peptide and permeation advisors; preservative, antioxidant, stability, and thickener recommenders; C.I. colorant reference (241 entries); oils & fats tables; sensory advisor (113 ingredients, 6-axis profile); INCI reverse-engineer for ingredient lookup.

**Ingredient Intelligence**
PubChem integration for molecular data, 2D structures, density, solubility, and LogP. pKa database with aqueous pH estimation. Allergen calculator covering 80+ EU allergens (Regulation 2023/1545). Stability checker (73 sensitive actives + 25 incompatibility pairs). Sensory advisor with target-vs-current suggestions. Salt-curve and solubilization indicators inline in the editor.

**Labels & Regulatory Reporting**
Product labels with auto-scaling fonts, custom sizes (15–200 mm), PAO icons, and A4 tiling. Export PDF/EPS. EU SUE Form A (Article 23, Reg. 1223/2009) PDF in 4 languages with mailto to 28 EU/EEA authorities. FDA MoCRA compliance fields (FEI registration, product listing, responsible person). FDA MedWatch Form 3500A for cosmetic adverse events with 15-day deadline tracking.

**Business Tools**
Projects with milestones, budgets, and journals. Accounting with income/expense, tax, recurring entries, and invoice attachments. Marketing plans with label profiles. Packaging inventory with stock tracking and reservations. Laboratory equipment library (20 equipment types, manual-PDF linking, dashboard alerts). Audit trail with field-level change tracking.

**Built for Privacy**
Your formulations are your intellectual property. All data stays on your device. iCloud sync is optional. Per-formulation AES-256-GCM encryption for trade secrets. App lock with password or biometrics. No telemetry. No third-party analytics.
